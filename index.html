What this prototype does

Background: AI foreground/background segmentation.

Typography: OCR finds text regions, then a local pixel-mask heuristic extracts likely lettering.

Text background: expanded OCR regions minus the lettering mask.

Decoration: segmented foreground minus detected text regions.

Edges: alpha masks are softly blurred to reduce jagged boundaries.

Model note

This prototype uses Transformers.js + BiRefNet for foreground segmentation and Tesseract.js for local OCR. RMBG-1.4 is source-available and its current model card specifies non-commercial use; check its licence before distributing a commercial version.
